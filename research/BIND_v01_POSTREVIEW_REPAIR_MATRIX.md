## Frozen stop-gate interpretation, pending prospective acceptance

1. **G1 SOURCE, 32 attempts, >=16 bilateral-competent pairs.** No BIND treatment until source provenance + honest pair feasibility and independent design review are established. Technical errors must be reported distinctly from scientific ineligibility; technical failure cannot justify outcome-conditioned redraw.
2. **G2 CAUSAL, reciprocal A→B and B→A at least +20pp** donor-specific *first-action-excluded* later-choice difference vs strongest identity-destroying null; terminal-success loss at most 10pp against PASSIVE; ordered action sets and no execution/privilege differences. This is a development screen, not proof by itself.
3. **G3 STRONG NULL + UNTOUCHED, at least +10pp** vs strongest information/compute-matched admissible baseline, terminal-success loss at most 5pp, with precommitted paired confidence intervals and power adequate for the claimed effect. No post-confirmation tuning.

At `n=16`, four treatment-only wins vs zero control-only wins give a two-sided exact paired discordance `p=0.125`; passing a 20pp point-estimate screen does not imply a statistically confirmed effect. Favor explicit utility and negative-result boundaries.
